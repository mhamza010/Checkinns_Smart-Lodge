Help & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a room?
To book a room, select your hotel and follow the booking instructions on the hotel's detail page.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and select local payment options.
How can I cancel my booking?
You can cancel your booking from your account dashboard or by contacting support at help@checkinns.com.
What is the check-in/check-out time?
Check-in is generally from 2:00 PM and check-out by 12:00 PM. Specific times may vary per property.
How do I contact support?
You can reach us at 0800 990 09292 or email support@checkinns.com.

About CheckInns

CheckInns provides seamless booking for hotels, restaurants and lounges worldwide. Our mission is to offer privacy, speed and security to all travellers.
